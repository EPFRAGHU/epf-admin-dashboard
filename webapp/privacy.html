1. What We Collect

At signup, we collect your name, email address, mobile number, and (for Employer accounts) establishment details you provide. Your password is hashed immediately and never stored or transmitted in plain text.

2. Employee Data

Once approved, any employee master, wage, and statutory filing data you enter is stored to enable EPF compliance reporting for your establishment(s) and is not shared with other accounts on the platform.

3. Review Process

Signup requests are reviewed manually by a platform administrator before an account is activated. Rejected requests do not result in an account being created.

4. Data Retention

Establishment and employee records are retained for as long as your account remains active, consistent with applicable statutory record-keeping requirements.

5. Contact

For questions about how your data is handled, contact your platform administrator.
