# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [DATE]
**Last Updated:** [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Solutions Services ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information through the EPF Admin Dashboard platform (the "Service").

**Important note on the data we process:** Unlike most software services, the Service is used by Consultants to manage Provident Fund compliance data on behalf of Establishments and their Employees. This means we process personal data of individuals (Employees) who do not directly sign up for or interact with the Service themselves. This policy addresses both categories of data: information about our direct account holders (Consultants), and information Consultants input about Establishments and Employees.

## 1. Who This Policy Covers

- **Consultants** — individuals or firms who create an account to use the Service.
- **Establishments** — the employer entities a Consultant manages within the Service.
- **Employees** — individuals whose PF-related data (entered by a Consultant on behalf of an Establishment) is processed within the Service.

If you are an Employee whose data appears in this system because your employer's consultant uses this Service, you did not create this account and did not choose to use this Service directly — your data is provided to us by the Consultant/Establishment for the sole purpose of PF compliance processing, as described below.

## 2. Information We Collect

### From Consultants (account holders)
- Name, mobile number, email address
- Login credentials (passwords are stored in encrypted/hashed form — we never store your password in readable form)
- Payment and billing information, processed through our third-party payment gateway (we do not store your full card or bank details ourselves)
- Usage data (login activity, actions taken within the Service) for security and support purposes

### About Establishments (entered by Consultants)
- Establishment name, PF code, address, coverage date
- Branch/division/unit structure, where applicable

### About Employees (entered by Consultants, on behalf of Establishments)
- Name, Universal Account Number (UAN), Member ID, father's/spouse's name
- Date of birth, date of joining, date of leaving (where applicable)
- Monthly wage figures, EPF/EPS/EDLI contribution data
- Gender, and other fields relevant to statutory EPF forms, where entered

We collect Employee data **only as input by the Consultant** — we do not independently collect or verify this data from any other source.

## 3. How We Use This Information

- To provide the core functionality of the Service: storing your data, performing PF contribution calculations, and generating statutory documents (ECR, Form 3A/6A/9/12A/5/10) for the Establishments you manage
- To process subscription fee billing, calculated based on Establishment and employee data you enter
- To communicate with you about your account, billing, and material changes to the Service
- To maintain security, prevent misuse, and enforce our Terms of Service
- To improve the Service (in aggregated or de-identified form wherever possible)

**We do not use Employee data for any purpose beyond providing the Service to the Consultant/Establishment that entered it.** We do not sell any data — Consultant, Establishment, or Employee — to third parties, and we do not use Employee data for marketing.

## 4. How We Store and Protect Data

- Data is stored on cloud database infrastructure (currently Neon/PostgreSQL) accessed only through our application's secure backend.
- Each Consultant's data is logically isolated — one Consultant cannot access another Consultant's Establishments or Employee data. Access controls are enforced at the application and database level.
- Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing (bcrypt or equivalent) — never in plain text.
- Payment credentials are handled by our payment processor (Cashfree Payments); we do not store full card numbers or bank account credentials on our own servers.
- We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

## 5. Who Can Access the Data

- **The Consultant** who entered the data, for their own Establishments only.
- **Platform administrators (superadmin)**, for the limited purposes of operating the platform, providing support, verifying subscription payments, and ensuring compliance with these policies — not for any unrelated commercial use.
- We do not share Establishment or Employee data with any third party except: (a) our payment processor, solely for billing purposes relating to the Consultant's subscription; (b) service providers who host our infrastructure (e.g. our database and hosting provider), bound by their own confidentiality/security obligations; or (c) where required by law.

## 6. Data Retention

- We retain Consultant, Establishment, and Employee data for as long as the Consultant's account remains active, and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with legal, tax, or audit requirements.
- A Consultant may request deletion of their account and associated data; certain records may be retained where required by applicable law (e.g., financial/billing records).
- If you are an Employee and wish to have your data corrected or removed, please raise this with the Consultant/Establishment who manages your PF records, as they control what data is entered — we can assist with technical execution of such a request once directed by the account holder responsible for that data.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you. Consultants can exercise these rights directly through the Service for their own account data. For requests relating to Employee data, please route the request through the Establishment/Consultant who manages that data, or contact us at the email below and we will assist in coordinating the request.

## 8. Children's Data

The Service is not directed at individuals under 18. Employee date-of-birth data may occasionally reflect individuals nearing statutory age thresholds (e.g., age 58 for EPS purposes) but this is standard PF compliance data, not data collected from or about children using the Service.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified within the Service or by email to Consultants.
